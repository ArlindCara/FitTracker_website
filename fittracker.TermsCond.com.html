Privacy Policy

Overview

At FitTracker we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Camera and pose data

The camera is used only to detect your body joints in real time and count your repetitions. The video feed is processed on your device as it happens: no video or image is ever recorded, saved or shared. Only the resulting joint angles are used, and only to track your exercise.

Information we collect

  • Personal identification information (username, email address, and the profile details you choose to provide such as date of birth, gender, weight and experience level).
  • Training data you enter or complete in the app (exercises, sets, reps, weight, rest, completion status).
  • Device information.
  • Usage data and analytics.

How we use your information

  • Provide the service: schedules, progress statistics, personal records and leaderboards.
  • Personalise your experience.
  • Analyse app performance and improve our application.
  • Send notifications such as reminders and progress updates, which you can disable at any time.

Leaderboard and visibility

Appearing on the leaderboard is optional. You can choose in the app settings whether to participate and whether your username is displayed.

Data protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Accounts are managed through Google Firebase Authentication, email addresses must be verified, and you can enable a biometric lock (fingerprint or face) on your device. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, use the Help Center section in the app (Settings › Help Center) or write to cararlind@gmail.com. Once an account deletion request is confirmed, all your data is permanently removed.
