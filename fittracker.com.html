AI Coach

A camera that understands how you move

FitTracker tracks your body joints 10 times per second with Google ML Kit pose detection and turns them into reps, form scores and live feedback — without ever saving a single frame.

Detect

On-device pose detection finds 33 body landmarks in every processed frame.

Measure

Landmarks become joint angles, cleaned and smoothed to remove noise.

Count

A rep counts only with full range of motion, proper tempo and symmetric limbs.

Score

Each rep is matched to the ideal movement and scored from 0 to 100.

Feedback that actually helps

Instead of a generic "bad rep", FitTracker finds the single joint angle that deviated the most from good technique and tells you what to correct.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. FitTracker is free to download and every feature — including the AI coach, Plan Wizard and progress analytics — is included.

Does the camera record me?

No. The camera is used only to detect your body joints in real time. The video feed is processed on your device as it happens: no video or image is ever recorded, saved or shared. Only the resulting joint angles are used, and only to track your exercise.

Where should I place my phone?

Make sure your whole body is visible. Each exercise shows a placement hint — front, side or at an angle — based on the views the AI was trained on. FitTracker detects your camera angle automatically.

How does the AI decide if a rep is good?

Each exercise has a "normality profile" built from correctly executed reps, recorded from several viewpoints. Your rep is compared with the closest view and scored 0–100; the joint that deviates the most becomes your correction tip. Movements far from any correct execution are not counted.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Pose detection runs on your device, and your schedule and progress are cached locally and synced automatically when you're back online.

Will I appear on the leaderboard?

Only if you want to. Leaderboard participation and username visibility can be turned on or off in the app settings.

Is FitTracker available on iPhone?

FitTracker is currently available on Android through Google Play. An iOS version is planned — subscribe to updates via the contact form to be notified.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Go to Settings › Help Center and submit a deletion request. Once confirmed, all your data is permanently removed.
